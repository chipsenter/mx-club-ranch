## Platform
web

## Stack
Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind; match jlsolutions-web. Static output. Cloudflare Pages is the working deployment assumption after the user accepted the proposed setup.

## Product Purpose
MX Club Ranch is a single-page motocross training website. Visitors can find training, upcoming and past events, and contact/location information.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Photo hero carousel; logo header with dropdown section navigation; booking calendar integration; upcoming and past event entries with image and text below; contact section; footer with map, address, phone, email, Facebook and Instagram. Keep the page simple and modern.

## Brand Commitments
Name: MX Club Ranch. Supplied logo: IMG_6089.png, copied to public/images/club-ranch-logo.png. Navy and cream proposal accepted; proceed directly from the logo and brief.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the logo is supplied. Photos, real event records, dates, training prices, address, phone, email, social URLs and booking account remain open. Never fabricate these facts. Any stock image must be described as illustrative in the content notes. Booking cannot accept reservations until a real provider link is configured.

## Users
Working assumption: motocross riders and families exploring training and planning visits.
